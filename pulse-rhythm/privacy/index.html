Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026. Applies to the Pulse Rhythm app for Apple Watch, published by Anthony Luo.

The short version

Pulse Rhythm does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections.

What stays on your watch

The app keeps a small amount of game state on the watch itself, in the app's own storage, so the game can remember where you are:

  • Best scores and grades for each mode and difficulty.
  • Journey progress — which sections you have completed and how many exercises.
  • Your settings: the beat channel (sound or haptic) and the beat guide.
  • Simple practice statistics that the game uses to pick which pattern to offer next.

None of this leaves the device. It is not tied to your name, Apple ID, or any identifier, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Settings includes a "Reset bests" control that clears scores on demand.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sent to us or to anyone else — there is no server.
  • No third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporters, or ad networks are included.
  • No access to the microphone, health data, location, contacts, or motion sensors.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no cookies.
  • No data is collected from children or anyone else; the app is suitable for all ages.

Apple

The app is distributed through the App Store. Apple may process information such as your purchase and, if you opt in on your device, crash and usage diagnostics, under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from Apple.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding an optional online feature — this page will be updated first and the effective date changed, and the app's App Store privacy label will say so.

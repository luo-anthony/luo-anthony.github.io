Pulse Rhythm

A rhythm game and trainer for Apple Watch. Read a written rhythm, tap it back against an always-on click, chase a score.

The game

Pulse Rhythm runs entirely on the watch — no iPhone app, no account, no network. The metronome never stops: you read the notation for as long as you like, then join on any downbeat and tap the rhythm on the surface below the line. Only timing counts.

Four ways to play: Rhythm Run (read a bar, tap it, climb a ladder from quarter notes to triplets and 6/8, with a tempo that ramps as you clear bars), Endless (sight-read a stream of bars with no pause), Journey (guided sections with a quiz gating each next one), and Conductor (hold a tempo from memory, graded in words). The beat can be a click or a tap on the wrist.

Support

The click is silent. watchOS Silent Mode mutes the app's sound. Turn Silent Mode off, or switch the beat to HAPTIC on the home screen — each beat then taps your wrist and the beat pips on the line carry the count.

Taps feel late or early. The grading window is ±180 ms around each note, measured from the moment your finger lands. Bluetooth headphones add delay; the app compensates for the audio route it can see, but a very high-latency headset can still feel off — try the watch speaker or the haptic beat.

A section is locked. Journey opens each section when you pass the previous one's quiz (three of four).

Anything else — bugs, ideas, a rhythm that felt unfair — email luo.anthony47@gmail.com. Please include your watch model and watchOS version.

Privacy

Pulse Rhythm collects no data. Scores and settings stay on your watch.
